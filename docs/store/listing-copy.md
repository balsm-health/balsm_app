# Store listing copy — Balsm patient app

Source of truth for App Store, Google Play and AppGallery text, live since 0.9.0. The
files under `fastlane/metadata/` hold the same text per store and locale; change both together.

Voice: calm, warm, second person, sentence case. No emoji, no exclamation marks, no
"download now". Don't over-sell features (languages, RTL), and don't mention open source.

Limits: App Store name and subtitle 30, promotional text 170, keywords 100, description
4000; Play title 30, short description 80, full description 4000; AppGallery brief introduction 80.

## English

**Name** (all stores): `Balsm – Your Health Story`  
**Subtitle** (App Store): `Care for You & Yours`  
**Short description** (Play, AppGallery brief introduction): `Care for You & Yours: your health story, kept close, even offline`  
**Keywords** (App Store): `medical,patient,prescription,lab,results,offline,doctor,clinic,history,file,document,arabic,health`

**Promotional text** (App Store)

```
Care for You & Yours. Your records, medicines and the care around you, kept safely on your phone and with you even without internet.
```

**Description** (App Store, Play, AppGallery)

```
Every visit, every prescription, every result is part of your story. Balsm keeps it together, close to you, for as long as you need it.

Your health, remembered
Your records, lab results and scans in one calm place. Note how you feel each day, and watch your blood pressure, glucose and weight take shape over time. When a doctor asks "since when?", the answer is in your hand.

Medicines, on time
Gentle reminders for every dose, and a clear history of what you took and when.

For the ones you love
Keep a profile for each person in your family, and follow a pregnancy week by week.

Ready when it matters
Your emergency card carries your blood type, allergies, conditions and the people to call. Show it, or let someone scan it, in the moments that count.

Care, close by
Pharmacies, clinics and labs near you, on a map of more than 34,000 places across Egypt.

Yours, and only yours
Your medicines and health records stay on your phone, encrypted, and are never sold. Balsm works without internet, and you can export everything at any time.

Care for You & Yours.

Balsm helps you keep track of your health. It does not replace your doctor's advice.
```

**What's new in 0.9.0**

```
This update is about the small moments: fixing a reading, finding a day, keeping what matters close.

• Family profiles and pregnancy tracking, week by week
• Edit a reading or record you already logged
• Delete medications, prescriptions and readings, one or several at once
• Reports grouped by day, with every reading from that day
• Emergency numbers follow the country you are in
• Clearer camera and photo permission messages, and many fixes
```

**Description** (App Store, Play, AppGallery)

```
كل زيارة، وكل وصفة، وكل نتيجة تحليل، جزء من حكايتك. يجمعها بلسم في مكان واحد، قريبة منك، ومعك ما دمت تحتاجها.

صحتك، محفوظة في الذاكرة
سجلاتك ونتائج تحاليلك وأشعتك في مكان واحد هادئ. تسجيل يومي لمشاعرك، ومتابعة ضغط الدم والسكر والوزن وهي تتضح مع الوقت. وحين يسأل الطبيب «منذ متى؟»، تكون الإجابة بين يديك.

أدويتك في موعدها
تذكير لطيف بكل جرعة، وسجل واضح لما أُخذ ومتى.

لمن تحب
ملف لكل فرد في عائلتك، ومتابعة الحمل أسبوعاً بأسبوع.

جاهز حين يهم الأمر
بطاقة الطوارئ تحمل فصيلة دمك وحساسيتك وأمراضك المزمنة ومن يُتصل بهم. تُعرض أو تُمسح في اللحظات التي تُحسب.

الرعاية قريبة منك
صيدليات وعيادات ومعامل بالقرب منك، على خريطة تضم أكثر من ٣٤ ألف مكان في مصر.

لك وحدك
أدويتك وسجلاتك الصحية تبقى على هاتفك، مشفّرة، ولا تُباع أبداً. بلسم يعمل دون إنترنت، ويمكن تصدير كل شيء في أي وقت.

لك ولمن تحب.

بلسم يساعدك على متابعة صحتك، ولا يغني عن نصيحة طبيبك.
```
