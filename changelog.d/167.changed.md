Vitals entry (blood pressure, glucose, weight, SpO₂, pulse) refuses a value that cannot be real, such as a mistyped `999`. Digits typed on an Arabic or Persian keyboard are read as numbers in the phone, date-of-birth and health-profile fields, and email addresses are checked for shape before sign-in. Under the hood the app moves to Flutter 3.50, Riverpod 3 and go_router 18, and every screen reads one shared set of colours, corner radii and type sizes.
