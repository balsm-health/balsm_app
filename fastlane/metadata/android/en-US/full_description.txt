Balsm keeps your health record where it belongs: on your phone.

Medications, dose history, prescriptions and health records are stored on your
device, encrypted. They are not uploaded, not synced to our servers, and not
shared. We do not track you, we do not advertise, and we do not measure how
you use the app. Balsm reports crashes so we can fix them, with your health
data stripped out before anything is sent.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Keep a medication list with dose history you actually record as you take it
• Photograph prescriptions and health records and keep them organised
• Build a care team of the doctors, clinics and pharmacies you use
• Carry an emergency card for the information someone would need in a crisis
• Find clinics and pharmacies near you
• Keep separate profiles for the people you care for

ARABIC AND ENGLISH
Balsm is built for both, not translated into one. The entire interface mirrors
to right-to-left in Arabic, with Arabic typography throughout.

WHAT BALSM IS NOT
Balsm does not diagnose, does not give medical advice, and does not replace
your doctor. It is a record-keeping tool for adults managing their own care.

YOUR DATA
Your account (name, email, country) and your care-team entries reach our
servers so your account works across devices. Your medications, doses and
records do not. You can delete your account, and everything with it, from
inside the app at any time.
