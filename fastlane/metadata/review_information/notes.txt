Balsm is a personal health record for patients in Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

SETUP NOTES
- At sign-up the app asks for an account country. Please choose Egypt.
  Balsm launches in Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the UAE only; the list is
  intentionally short and is not a defect.
- The app requires users to be 18 or over. Please enter a date of birth
  before 2008 if prompted.
- The app is bilingual. Switch between English and Arabic in
  Settings > Language; the whole interface mirrors to right-to-left.

WHERE THINGS ARE
- Medications and dose history: Medications tab.
- Health records and prescription photos: Records tab.
- Care team, including import from your contacts: Care Team tab.
- Nearby clinics and pharmacies: Care Team > Find care. This is the only
  feature that uses location.
- Account deletion: Settings > Account > Delete account. Deletion is
  immediate and in-app.

PRIVACY
Medications, dose history and health records are stored only on the device,
encrypted with SQLCipher. There is no server endpoint for them. Account
identity, care-team entries and the nearby-care search do reach our API, and
are declared in the privacy questionnaire. The app contains no analytics or
advertising SDK and does no tracking.
